# Playing & Moving Cards

<p className="help-lede">The commands that move cards around the table: playing, drawing, discarding, burning, moving, and shuffling.</p>

## Playing Cards & Influencing

`play` plays a card from your hand; `influence` plays a vampire from your uncontrolled region. Both add context to the game chat and set up the card's initial state — capacity, disciplines, and so on.

<Callout title="Tip">
  Clicking a card name in hand (or another playable region) shows the list of modes it can be played with. Cards with multiple modes show multiple options, and picking one that uses disciplines prints those disciplines to chat automatically. A card that needs a target prompts for it once you've picked the mode. Sensible default target regions are already wired up, though not every card has been mapped correctly yet.
</Callout>

## Drawing Cards

`draw` is the best way to draw — `move` can technically do the same thing, but `draw` writes clearer context to the game chat.

<Callout title="Tip">
  Quick Commands has one-click shortcuts for drawing a single card from the library or crypt.
</Callout>

## Discarding Cards

`discard` removes a card from your hand only, with the option to pick it randomly and to redraw immediately or not.

<Callout title="Tip">
  Quick Commands has a shortcut for discarding a random card. Clicking a card in hand also offers "discard" and "discard and redraw" shortcuts directly.
</Callout>

## Burning Cards

`burn` moves a card from the ready/torpor region into its owner's ash heap.

<Callout title="Tip">
  Clicking a card in play offers a "burn" shortcut.
</Callout>

## Moving Cards

For everything else, `move` relocates a card from one position (`PLAYER`/`REGION`/`CARD`) to another. It's the command you'll use most, and covers nearly every situation a game of VTES can produce.

<Callout title="Tip">
  There's currently no click-to-move — this one's command-only. Valid destination regions are `ready`, `uncontrolled`, `hand`, `library`, `crypt`, `ashheap`, `torpor`, and `rfg` (use `rfg` for anything removed from the game). The keywords `prey` and `predator` are shortcuts for the destination player.
</Callout>

## Shuffling

`shuffle` shuffles a whole region, or randomizes just a portion of it.

<Callout title="Tip">
  Quick Commands has shortcuts to shuffle your library or crypt.
</Callout>

## Command Reference

<CommandDoc name="Play" syntax="play [SOURCE] [@ DISCIPLINES] [DESTINATION] [draw]" description="Play a card from your hand or another region such as research, with optional context for which disciplines it's played with and whether to draw a replacement.">
  <CommandOption name="SOURCE">Defaults to your own hand.</CommandOption>
  <CommandOption name="@ DISCIPLINES">Follow the <code>@</code> keyword with one or more discipline codes to tell other players which mode you're playing the card in.</CommandOption>
  <CommandOption name="DESTINATION">A player/region moves the card into that region; a player/region/card moves it onto an existing card. Defaults to your own ash heap.</CommandOption>
  <CommandOption name="draw">Draws a replacement card immediately.</CommandOption>
  <CommandExample cmd="play 2 draw">Move hand #2 to your ash-heap and redraw (combat cards).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="play 3">Move hand #3 to your ash-heap without redrawing (Bum's Rush, Wake with Evening's Freshness).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="play 2 ready draw">Move hand #2 to your ready region and replace (allies).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="play 1 ready 2 1">Move hand #1 onto card #2 in your ready region (retainers, equipment).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="play 1 Lilith uncontrolled 1">Move hand #1 onto card #1 in Lilith's uncontrolled region (Brainwash).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="play 1 Lilith ready 2">Move hand #1 onto card #2 in Lilith's ready region (Fame).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="play 1 @ ani">Play hand #1 to your ash-heap, printing the Animalism icon in chat.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="play 1 @ ani,FOR">Play hand #1 to your ash-heap, printing the Animalism and superior Fortitude icons in chat.</CommandExample>
</CommandDoc>

<CommandDoc name="Influence" syntax="influence [SOURCE]" description="Play a vampire from your uncontrolled region, adjusting capacity and disciplines as required.">
  <CommandOption name="SOURCE">Always your own uncontrolled region — there's no PLAYER or REGION to specify.</CommandOption>
  <CommandExample cmd="influence 2">Play uncontrolled #2 into your ready region.</CommandExample>
</CommandDoc>

<CommandDoc name="Draw" syntax="draw [crypt] [AMOUNT]" description="Draw cards from your library into your hand, or from your crypt into your uncontrolled region.">
  <CommandOption name="crypt">Draws from your crypt into your uncontrolled region instead of library into hand.</CommandOption>
  <CommandOption name="AMOUNT">Number of cards to draw.</CommandOption>
  <CommandExample cmd="draw">Draw 1 card from your library into your hand.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="draw crypt">Draw 1 card from your crypt into your uncontrolled region.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="draw 3">Draw 3 cards into your hand (Heart of Nizchetus).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="draw crypt 4">Draw 4 cards from your crypt into your uncontrolled region.</CommandExample>
</CommandDoc>

<CommandDoc name="Discard" syntax="discard [CARD | random] [draw]" description="Move a card from your hand to the ash-heap. Doesn't draw a replacement unless told to.">
  <CommandOption name="CARD">Position of the card in your hand.</CommandOption>
  <CommandOption name="random">Pick a random card from your hand.</CommandOption>
  <CommandOption name="draw">Draw a replacement card immediately.</CommandOption>
  <CommandExample cmd="discard 3">Discard card #3 from your hand without replacing it.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="discard 5 draw">Discard card #5 from your hand and replace it from your library.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="discard random">Discard a random card from hand without replacing it.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="discard random draw">Discard a random card from hand and replace it.</CommandExample>
</CommandDoc>

<CommandDoc name="Burn" syntax="burn [SOURCE]" description="Burn a card, moving it to its owner's ash heap. Any cards stacked on it are also burned to their own owners' ash heaps.">
  <CommandOption name="SOURCE">Defaults to your own ready region.</CommandOption>
  <CommandExample cmd="burn ready 2">Burn card #2 in your ready region — useful for master cards that come into play.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="burn Lilith ready 2">Burn card #2 in Lilith's ready region — useful for destroying a rival's location or equipment.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="burn Lilith library 1">Burn the top card of Lilith's library (The Slaughterhouse).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="burn crypt 1">Burn the top card of your own crypt.</CommandExample>
</CommandDoc>

<CommandDoc name="Move" syntax="move [SOURCE] [DESTINATION] [top]" description="Move any card from one position to another.">
  <CommandOption name="SOURCE">Defaults to your own ready region.</CommandOption>
  <CommandOption name="DESTINATION">Defaults to your own ready region. Also accepts the <code>prey</code>/<code>predator</code> keywords in place of a player name.</CommandOption>
  <CommandOption name="top">Moves the card to the top of the destination region/card instead of the bottom.</CommandOption>
  <CommandExample cmd="move 1">Move ready #1 to the end of your ready region — useful for re-ordering.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="move 2 1 ready 1">Move card #1 stacked on ready #2 onto ready #1 — for moving equipment/retainers between minions.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="move torpor 1 ready">Move torpor #1 into your ready region — rescuing from torpor.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="move Caine torpor 1 Lilith ready">Move Caine's torpor #1 into Lilith's ready region (Graverobbing).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="move hand 1 research">Move hand #1 into the research area — for cards that go face-down (Mokolé Blood).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="move ashheap 1 hand">Move ash-heap #1 into your hand (resolving Ashur Tablets).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="move ashheap 1 library">Move ash-heap #1 to the bottom of your library (recursion cards like Ashur Tablets).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="move ashheap 1 library top">Move ash-heap #1 to the top of your library — for cards that re-sort your library.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="move library 1 hand">Move the top card of your library into your hand (Heart of Nizchetus).</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="move 1 prey ready">Move your card #1 into your prey's ready region.</CommandExample>
</CommandDoc>

<CommandDoc name="Shuffle" syntax="shuffle [SOURCE] [AMOUNT]" description="Shuffle a stack of cards.">
  <CommandOption name="SOURCE">Defaults to your own library.</CommandOption>
  <CommandOption name="AMOUNT">Number of cards to shuffle.</CommandOption>
  <CommandExample cmd="shuffle">Shuffle your library.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="shuffle Lilith library">Shuffle Lilith's library.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="shuffle crypt">Shuffle your crypt.</CommandExample>
  <CommandExample cmd="shuffle library 5">Shuffle only the top 5 cards of your library, leaving the rest of the order untouched.</CommandExample>
</CommandDoc>
